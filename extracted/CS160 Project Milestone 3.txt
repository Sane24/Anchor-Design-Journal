2) Critique Session with Group
Design 1 (Made by Na Mi):
Positive critique:
● Well-structured organization of tasks with the first step clearly displayed.
● Navigation bar icons are easy to distinguish.
● Strong emphasis on the three main tasks, reducing cognitive overload
Negative critique:
● Green and yellow color scheme feels somewhat jarring.
● Lacks a help or documentation section.
● Unclear which page is currently active because no navigation icon is highlighted
Design 2 (Made by Sampurn):

-- 3 of 11 --

Positive critique:
● Cohesive color palette using sage greens and neutral tones creates a calming,
wellness-focused aesthetic
● Effectively supports breaking tasks into smaller steps and clearly communicates user
progress
● Navigation icons combine symbols and text, improving clarity and discoverability.
Negative critique:
● The “Start 5 Minutes” and “Tomorrow” pills are unclear in purpose
● Small text, particularly in the welcome section, reduces readability
● “Add Today’s Tasks” is not clearly distinguishable from the “Start Morning Briefing”
button
Design 3 (Made by Madison):
Positive critique:
● Cohesive color palette with a clean, light interface.
● Centrally placed floating "+" button makes adding tasks intuitive.
● Clear task filter bar (All, To-Do, In Progress, Completed) supports efficient task
management.
Negative critique:
● Notification badges can be visually distracting.
● The morning screen begins with multiple colorful mood reflection icons, which may
compete for attention.
● Missing key productivity features such as task breakdowns, focus/sprint sessions, and the
ability to check off completed tasks.
Why we selected design 2:
We selected Design 2 as our direction because it best aligned with our goal of helping
overwhelmed students with mental health conditions manage their workload in a calm,
approachable way. We thought the cohesive color palette would ensure a calming aesthetic and
could be applied if we implemented the Growth Garden, which is one of our stretch goals. We
also thought this design emphasized breaking larger tasks into smaller, more manageable steps
with a visual anchor for focus time, which we hope to keep for our future prototype. For the next
iteration, we plan to address some usability issues like improving the clarity of the “Start 5
Minutes” and “Tomorrow” buttons. We also wanted to create a stronger visual distinction
between the “Add Today’s Tasks” and “Start Morning Briefing.”
