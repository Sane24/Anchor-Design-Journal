1. Introduction & Overview
1.1 Project Overview & Intended Users
Imagine walking out of an important lecture right after your professor
announces a quiz for the next class. You rush to the nearest library, find a
table, set down your phone and iPad, and adjust your laptop screen to 110°
only to freeze. Between revising for the upcoming quiz, finishing other
assignments across multiple classes, and managing commitments outside
of school, you do not even know where to begin, and doomscrolling will not
help you progress any further.
Introducing to you, Anchor, an application that is built to help you in these
times of decision fatigue. Instead of being overwhelmed with listing your
endless amount of tasks and having them lost after, Anchor operates on a
simple, grounded philosophy: you will
steady the day by limiting your daily
focus to 3 core tasks, or what we like to call, “Anchors.”
As you constrain your daily targets to what matters most on the current
day, Anchor will break the paralysis of what to start, reduce cognitive
overload, and help turn your stressful and chaotic study sessions into
calm, intentional progress.
Intended Users: Students, recent graduates, and independent learners
looking for a minimalist, low-friction task management tool that prioritizes
clarity, focus, and daily reflection over endless accumulation of stress.

3.5 Managing Integration & Preferences
Figure 7: Settings and Preferences Page
1. Tap the Settings tab from the bottom navigation

needed
3. Under appearance, tap a color swatch to change your accent theme

4. Known Limitations &
Troubleshooting
4.1 Known Limitations
1. Anchor is currently optimized for mobile viewports (390px width). The
implementation for a dedicated desktop layout is in progress
2. Gmail messages are filtered using a fixed set of rules to identify
"task-like" emails; this filtering is not yet user-customizable.
3. "Continue with Google" sign-in is in progress. A combined Google
sign-in (linking account creation with Calendar/Gmail access in one
step) is still being finalized and may not be fully available.
4. Cafe and Rain sounds during the focus timer need some
improvement.
5. Email verification is not enforced. New accounts are not currently
required to verify their email address before signing in.
6. Account deletion has not been implemented.
7. Forget password leads to a blank screen on the app, as we are still
making adjustments to Supabase.
8. Anchor limit is fixed. Users can select a maximum of 3 Anchors per
day, and this limit is not currently adjustable.
9. Notifications are blocked by the browser, unless the tab has been
granted permission.

4.3 FAQs
1) Can other users see my tasks or progress?
No. Your plans, reflections, and focus sessions are private to your
account.
2) What happens to tasks I don't complete?
They roll over automatically and appear as suggestions in the next
day's Morning Briefing.
3) Can I add another task after having three added already?
No. You can only add at most 3 tasks, as we don’t want you to be
overwhelmed with tasks.
